# ALIGN Design System
## Product
ALIGN is a modern public-impact financial guidance platform that helps marginalized entrepreneurs discover, understand, compare, and apply for suitable government-backed financial schemes.
The experience should feel trustworthy, human, approachable, premium, and accessible.
It should NOT look like:
- A traditional government portal
- A generic banking website
- A generic AI SaaS dashboard
- A futuristic AI product
- A startup landing page filled with gradients and decorative effects
## Design Philosophy
Core qualities:
- Trustworthy
- Human
- Clear
- Accessible
- Calm
- Modern
- Premium
- Helpful
Prioritize information clarity over decoration.
The interface should make users feel:
"I understand my options, I can trust the information, and I know what to do next."
## Visual Direction
Use:
- Warm neutral backgrounds
- Charcoal typography
- Restrained green and blue accents
- Generous whitespace
- Clear visual hierarchy
- Subtle borders
- Very light shadows
- Moderate corner rounding
- Clean modern sans-serif typography
- Simple line icons
- Subtle motion and micro-interactions
Avoid:
- Neon colors
- Excessive gradients
- Glassmorphism
- Glowing effects
- Excessive rounded pills
- Floating blobs
- Futuristic interfaces
- Excessive illustrations
- Overly dense dashboards
## Color System
Primary background:
Warm off-white / ivory.
Primary text:
Deep charcoal.
Secondary text:
Muted charcoal / gray.
Primary action:
Deep, restrained blue.
Secondary accent:
Muted natural green.
Green should communicate:
- Eligible
- Recommended
- Success
- Positive financial outcomes
Blue should communicate:
- Primary actions
- Information
- Navigation
- Trust
Use red only for genuine errors or important warnings.
Keep colors restrained and professional.
## Typography
Use a highly readable modern sans-serif font.
Typography should have:
- Strong hierarchy
- Comfortable line height
- Clear numerical typography
- Excellent readability for financial information
Suggested hierarchy:
Display:
Large, confident, minimal.
H1:
Strong but approachable.
H2:
Clear section headings.
H3:
Card and component headings.
Body:
Highly readable and comfortable.
Small:
Supporting information, metadata, and sources.
Numbers:
Use clear, prominent typography for loan amounts, interest rates, EMI, tenure, and other financial values.
## Spacing
Use a consistent spacing system.
Favor generous whitespace.
Desktop layouts should generally use:
- Wide content areas
- Strong alignment
- Comfortable vertical spacing
- Clear separation between sections
Avoid cramped layouts.
## Components
### Buttons
Primary:
Solid dark blue, white text.
Secondary:
White or warm neutral surface with subtle border.
Tertiary:
Text-based action.
Buttons should be clear and purposeful.
Avoid excessive pill-shaped buttons.
### Cards
Use cards for:
- Recommended schemes
- Financial summaries
- Eligibility information
- Partner information
- Documents
- AI guidance
Cards should have:
- Moderate corner radius
- Subtle border
- Minimal shadow
- Clear internal spacing
Avoid cards inside cards unless necessary.
### Inputs
Inputs should feel simple and approachable.
Use:
- Clear labels
- Comfortable height
- Visible focus state
- Helpful placeholder text
- Clear validation messages
The AI need-analysis input should be a major interaction, not a tiny form field.
### Status Badges
Use restrained badges for:
- Eligible
- Recommended
- Best match
- Not eligible
- Information
Do not overuse badges.
### Navigation
Use a simple, clean navigation system.
The product should feel like a modern financial/public-impact platform, not an enterprise administration system.
### Progress
The main journey should visually communicate progress:
Need Analysis
→ Scheme Matching
→ Compare
→ Simulate
→ Select
→ Find Partner
→ Apply
Use subtle progress indicators rather than complicated steppers.
## Financial Information
Financial numbers are extremely important.
Make these easy to scan:
- Maximum loan
- Interest rate
- Loan amount
- EMI
- Repayment period
- Moratorium
- Total repayment
Use strong typography and clear labels.
Never make financial information visually confusing.
## AI Interaction
ALIGN uses AI primarily to understand natural-language user needs, personalize scheme recommendations, explain financial outcomes, and answer scheme-specific questions.
AI UI should feel:
- Helpful
- Human
- Calm
- Transparent
Avoid presenting AI as magical or futuristic.
When AI produces a recommendation, show the reasons behind it.
Example:
"Recommended because:
✓ Your income falls within the scheme limit
✓ Your requested amount fits the loan limit
✓ The scheme supports your business purpose"
## Trust & Transparency
Source information should be visible where appropriate.
Government scheme information should include a subtle source/reference treatment.
Use language such as:
"Source: Official scheme guidelines"
Do not make the interface look bureaucratic.
## Maps
Partner discovery should use a clean map interface with:
- Simple map
- Clear location markers
- Nearby partner cards
- Distance
- Eligibility/status information
The map should support the decision rather than dominate the page.
## Motion
Use subtle motion only.
Examples:
- Smooth page transitions
- Button hover states
- Slider transitions
- Card reveal
- Loading states
Avoid dramatic animations.
## Responsive Design
Design primarily for desktop presentation but ensure the system can adapt naturally to tablet and mobile layouts.
## Overall Experience
ALIGN should feel like:
"Someone finally made government financial assistance easy to understand."
It should combine the trust of a financial institution with the simplicity of a modern consumer product.
Every screen should answer:
1. What am I looking at?
2. Why does this matter to me?
3. What should I do next?
