How to proceed (Application Journey)

Follow this sequence to ensure zero-rejection routing.

Step 01 Prepare & certify documents

Gather 2 self-attested photocopies and keep DigiLocker copies ready for Aadhaar, Caste, and DPR machinery quotation.

Step 02 Initiate KVIC Online e-Portal Routing

Submit basic personal profile on the official PMEGP online portal designating 'State Bank of India, SME Park Street' as preferred financing branch.

Step 03 Visit SBI SME Park Street Nodal Desk

Present your ALIGN Dossier and hardcopy documents directly to Officer Shri S. Chatterjee at Counter 04 for formal intake receipt.

Step 04 DLTFC Committee Scrutiny

Branch forwards project to District Level Task Force Committee; technical field verification completed within 7–14 working days.

Step 05 Sanction & Subsidy Escrow Release

Loan disbursed to machinery vendor; ₹42,000 (35% margin money) locked into 3-year term deposit escrow as per KVIC guidelines.

Procedures subject to State Level Bankers' Committee (SLBC) West Bengal guidelines. Confirm branch rules at intake.
